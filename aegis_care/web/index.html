The CARE control loop

Four gates. One enforced recovery.

Candidate signals nominate. Counterfactual evidence authorises. Trusted state rebuilds. Enforcement keeps the recovery closed.

C
Candidate discovery
Visible lineage plus scoped fingerprints recover the missing-edge candidate set.
A
Attribution
Local counterfactual replay separates causality from surface similarity.
R
Recompilation
Confirmed descendants rebuild from trusted FHIR, or fail closed to quarantine.
E
Enforcement
Signed tombstones and resurrection probes keep withdrawn influence out.
Why this exists

Deleting the visible error does not delete what learned from it.

A wrong patient association can become a handover, then a summary, then a future retrieval cue. Full reset buys safety by erasing useful memory. Central forensic reconstruction buys accuracy by opening records the investigator may not be authorised to read. AEGIS-Care is the third path.

Privacy boundary

Move verdicts—not patient records.

Raw clinical content stays inside the owning runtime. The coordinator sees commitments, bounded signals, bands, counts, and signed dispositions.
